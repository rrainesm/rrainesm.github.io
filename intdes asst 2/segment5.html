Safeguarding against AI

Adopting the National Library Board's S.U.R.E. framework can identify tell-tale signs of AI misinformation.

S - Source: What are the origins? Are they trustworthy?

U - Understand: Do you know what you're reading? Search for clarity

R - Research: Dig deeper. What other sources are there?

E - Evaluate: Find the balance between angles. Exercise fair judgement.
